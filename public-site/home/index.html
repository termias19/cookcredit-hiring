Your home. Your way.

A clear plan, before anyone arrives.

1. Tell us what helps

Choose cooking, household cleaning, both or help deciding. You can arrange support for someone else with their permission.

2. Agree the visit

CookCredit reviews the address, date and helper availability. Regular help can be requested weekly, every two weeks or every four weeks; each future visit needs confirmation.

3. Review every cost

Your itemized quote shows work time, ingredients and materials, service fees and reviewed tax. Pay securely through Stripe after the agreed scope and full price are approved.

Small details make home feel right.

When arranging a visit, you can discuss ingredients to avoid, meal instructions you already follow, easy-to-chew meals, cleaning preferences and whether to use your own supplies. Share only the details needed for the service.

We provide household help, not medical care or dietary advice. An allergen-free kitchen cannot be guaranteed. Availability depends on the address, schedule and helper review.

Visit instructions, addresses and receipts stay in the private account. They are not published on this page.
