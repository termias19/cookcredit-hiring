# Production acceptance and role lifecycle release — 26 September 2026

Employers can now close and reopen their existing roles. Closed roles reject new applications and assessment submissions while preserving existing candidate evidence. Reopening enforces the approved five-role allowance under a workspace lock; concurrent requests cannot overrun it. Company access, verified account and seat permissions are enforced. Integration-managed roles remain managed by their integration.

The frontend router is pinned to 7.18.4 to address reported dependency vulnerabilities. The production dependency audit reports zero findings; development dependency advisories remain and are not represented as resolved.
